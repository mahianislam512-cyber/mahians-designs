Quick Guide How to use

🖼️ Photos: Upload your design images (JPG/PNG/WebP). Choose a category (Branding / Social / Print / UI). They show in the Portfolio section — 1 big on top, 2 side-by-side below, repeating.

🎬 Videos: Upload an MP4 or paste a YouTube / Vimeo link. Pick the tab (Motion Graphics / Video Edit / Graphics Designs) and ratio. Each tab shows 16:9 on top and 9:16 pairs below.

👤 Profile: Change your photo, name, intro text and stats.

📞 Contact: Update WhatsApp, Discord and phone links used across the whole site.

🌐 Social Media: Add YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Behance, Dribbble, LinkedIn, Fiverr and more — shown on your profile card, video section, contact and footer.
